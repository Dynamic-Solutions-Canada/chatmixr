- New: ChatMixr can switch to your TV, monitor or speakers when they turn on, and away from them when they turn off. Turn on "Switch devices when a TV, monitor or speaker turns on or off" in Settings or under Options in the tray popup. It only switches to a device that comes first in your order on the Devices page.
- The Game-to-Chat popup no longer appears on its own when a headset's ChatMix wheel shifts by a step or two. Turning the wheel still shows it.
- The German interface now addresses you as "du".
