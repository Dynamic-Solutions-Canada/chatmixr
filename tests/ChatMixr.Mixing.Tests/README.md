# Mixing rules harness

Checks the parts of mixing that don't need Windows. It links the app's `ReceiverReport.cs` (wheel, power, battery and status messages), `MixLevels.cs`, `ChatApps.cs` and `SessionVolumeTracker.cs` unchanged and runs them on Linux.

Covers: wheel reports captured from a real Nova 7X receiver (game and chat levels, the middle position), other reports on the same interface ignored, levels capped at 100; the volume curve's ends and shape; chat app matching by program name with or without `.exe` and in any case; and the volume bookkeeping: an app's first volume becomes its baseline and is scaled, the wheel back in the middle restores it, a change made in Windows' volume mixer becomes the new baseline, a change while the wheel has the app at zero is left alone, a baseline saved by a run that crashed wins over the scaled volume found at start, a second session of the same program shares its baseline, and a forgotten baseline is read afresh. Also the device rules: the slider (detent when dragged, a key press always leaving the middle), what Switch to moves (`DefaultRoles.cs`: calls and a headset's mic follow along only when they were with the old device, and a headset's mic left behind by speakers or a TV follows to the next headset (the switches in a real log), while a desk mic or the PC's own mic jack stays put; the menu's single roles; calls following an output change made outside ChatMixr), snapshot redraws when a device's roles change, the device order, shortcuts, when a wheel shows the mix popup (`MixPopup.cs`: a flicker of a step or two never does, a turn shows from its third step and follows smoothly, and flicker after a turn can't hold the popup open), the relay buffer (order, overflow, drift trimmed during quiet audio and past a ceiling) and silence detection (`RelaySilence.cs`), cable pairing, the test sounds, and chat app search. And the Game Bar widget's pipe messages (`WidgetProtocol.cs`): states and commands round-trip as one line each, and malformed, unknown, oversize or device-less commands are dropped; and the package sandbox SID that limits who may open that pipe (`AppContainerSid.cs`) matches the one Windows reported.

Does not prove: that the receivers are found and read, that Windows applies the volumes or defaults, how the relay sounds, which endpoint belongs to which headset, or that the curve sounds right; nor that the widget's pipe opens from Game Bar's sandbox or that the tray app acts on its commands. Those need Windows and the headsets; see the manual checks in [docs/development.md](../../docs/development.md).

Run with `npm test`, or alone with `external/winui-app-kit/scripts/dotnet run --project tests/ChatMixr.Mixing.Tests`.
